# Portfolyo

**AI-Powered Portfolio & CV Platform for the Swedish Job Market**
v1.0.0 — Created by Said Borna

---

### What it does

Portfolyo turns Swedish YH students and recent graduates into candidates who get noticed. It replaces Word-dokument, half-finished GitHub Pages, and generic website builders with a single guided experience: fill in your information, let AI refine your language, pick a visual direction — and publish to a personal URL in minutes. The platform produces portfolios and CVs that look like they were designed individually, not assembled from a template gallery. Over 150 design variants share a unified visual identity built on deliberate restraint and typographic precision. PDF export, AI-assisted copywriting adapted to Swedish professional conventions, and instant publishing at portfolyo.se come standard.

### Why it exists

Swedish YH students graduate into a job market that demands professional presentation, but every available tool was designed for someone else. LinkedIn is a social network, not a portfolio. Squarespace and Wix produce pages that signal "template" before they signal "competence." Building from scratch requires time and skills that students in intensive programs simply don't have — especially during LIA placement periods when first impressions determine career trajectories. Portfolyo closes that gap: a platform that understands the Swedish job market, speaks the language, and delivers output that competes with what a designer would charge thousands of kronor to produce.

### Current state

Portfolyo is under active development with a clear trajectory toward market launch. The core platform — authentication, guided onboarding, portfolio and CV creation, AI text generation, template rendering, PDF export, and public publishing — is functional and deployed at portfolyo.se. The security and infrastructure layer is production-grade. The ambition behind Portfolyo is not a side project or a student exercise. It is a market-ready SaaS product being built to compete seriously in the Swedish professional tools space, with the design rigor, technical foundation, and product vision to back that claim.
